"Hanabi" (fireworks) beautifully illuminate the summer night sky in Japan. This massive, colorful art of light blooming in the sky is an absolute must-see summer event for foreign tourists visiting Japan. Here is a list of recommended fireworks festivals held around Tokyo in July and August 2026 where you can easily enjoy spectacular views for free, along with local tips on how to make the most of them smartly!

## Top Recommended Hanabi Festivals

### 1. Sumidagawa Fireworks Festival

- **Date & Time:** July 25, 2026 (Sat), Scheduled from 19:00
- **Nearest Stations:** Asakusa, Kuramae, Tokyo Skytree, Honjo-Azumabashi, etc.
- **Location:** Around the Sumida River (<https://maps.app.goo.gl/pBbvJm9nCbSCs1uK8>)

### 2. Katsushika Noryo Fireworks Festival

- **Date & Time:** July 28, 2026 (Tue), Scheduled from 19:20
- **Nearest Stations:** Shibamata, Kanamachi, Shin-Shibamata
- **Location:** Edogawa Riverbed / Shibamata Baseball Field (<https://maps.app.goo.gl/x5RsKBr93qaya5kN7>)

### 3. Edogawa Fireworks Festival

- **Date & Time:** August 1, 2026 (Sat), Scheduled from 19:15
- **Nearest Stations:** Shinozaki, Koiwa, Edogawa, Mizue
- **Location:** Edogawa Riverbed / Near Shinozaki Park (<https://maps.app.goo.gl/kYdhTkpcjpwG6gYk7>)

## Smart & Cheap! Local Tips to Enjoy Fireworks 120% for Free

Here are some "local techniques" to help you enjoy the fireworks completely free of charge and in absolute comfort!

**Bring a "Leisure Sheet" from a 100-Yen Shop**

![](/images/columns/tokyo-free-fireworks-guide/01.webp)

To sit on the grass by the river, make sure to buy a thin vinyl picnic sheet (called a "leisure sheet" in Japan) at a 100-yen shop like Daiso or Seria. With an investment of just 100 yen, that spot turns into your own comfortable "Free VIP Seat." We also highly recommend picking up some antibacterial wipes, which are super handy when eating street food, and a handheld fan to beat the summer night heat—all available at 100-yen shops!

**Utilize Convenience Stores and Supermarkets**

![](/images/columns/tokyo-free-fireworks-guide/02.webp)

Buying *Yakisoba* or *Takoyaki* from the food stands at the venue is fun, but the prices can be a bit high. If you want to keep expenses down, the smart local style is to stock up on cheap fried chicken (*Karaage*), rice balls (*Onigiri*), and chilled beer at a supermarket or convenience store before heading to the venue! Convenience stores not only have delicious hot snacks but also a wide variety of snacks starting at around 100 yen, making them perfect affordable additions to accompany your beer.

**Secure Your Spot While It's Still Light Out**

![](/images/columns/tokyo-free-fireworks-guide/03.webp)

Good free spots are strictly first-come, first-served! The best strategy is to arrive 1 to 2 hours before the fireworks start, lay out your sheet, and enjoy a picnic while watching the evening sky.
