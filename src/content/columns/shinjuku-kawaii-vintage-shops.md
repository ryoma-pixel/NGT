Tokyo's Shinjuku is at the forefront of cutting-edge Japanese fashion and the "Kawaii" (cute) culture that is taking the world by storm.

In our previous article, "[Top 10 Recommended Japanese Souvenirs Foreigners Will Love!](/column/best-japan-souvenir-ranking)", we introduced classic souvenirs. This time, we're bringing you a practical guide for those thinking, "I want cute Japanese character goods!" or "I want to buy stylish clothes and yukata cheaply, just like the youth in Tokyo!"

From megastores featuring globally beloved characters to stylish vintage clothing shops capturing the latest Japanese trends, we've carefully selected 4 highly recommended shops where you can fully experience "Kawaii" in Shinjuku!

#### 1. Nishikaigan Ruby’s Shinjuku Store

**Retro & Kawaii! A Hidden Gem for Cheap, Stylish Vintage Clothes and "Yukata~~"~~**

![](/images/columns/shinjuku-kawaii-vintage-shops/01.webp)![](/images/columns/shinjuku-kawaii-vintage-shops/02.webp)

"Vintage clothing" is a massive trend among Japanese youth right now. Located in the Central East Exit area of Shinjuku Station, "Nishikaigan Ruby's" is a hugely popular vintage shop where you can get girly, retro "Kawaii" items at reasonable prices. Look for the pink neon sign!

![](/images/columns/shinjuku-kawaii-vintage-shops/03.webp)

While they have great t-shirts and dresses, we especially recommend the "Yukata" that line the store as summer approaches! You can get these traditional Japanese summer kimonos at incredibly cheap prices (around a few thousand yen) thanks to them being secondhand. Snagging a cute yukata here and wearing it to a fireworks festival or a local *matsuri* is the ultimate local experience. (Note: Yukata might sell out, so keep that in mind!)

- **Theme:** Stylish Kawaii vintage clothes and affordable yukata
- **Store Name:** Nishikaigan Ruby's Shinjuku Store
- **Hours:** 12:00 - 21:00
- **Location:** Seven Building 1F, 3-36-14 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo
- **Google Maps:** <https://maps.app.goo.gl/p9ywGFEL8F6sUr8w6>

---

#### 2. Sanrio Shinjuku Store

**A Global Icon! Exclusive Japanese-Style "Hello Kitty" Goods**

![](/images/columns/shinjuku-kawaii-vintage-shops/04.webp)![](/images/columns/shinjuku-kawaii-vintage-shops/05.webp)

When you think of "Kawaii" culture, Sanrio characters like Hello Kitty are the ultimate icons. Among the Sanrio shops in the Shinjuku area, the "Sanrio Shinjuku Store," located on the 1st floor of the Kinokuniya Bookstore Main Store, boasts a massive selection and is an absolute must-visit spot for souvenir hunting.

![](/images/columns/shinjuku-kawaii-vintage-shops/06.webp)

While retro and cute t-shirts and tote bags for everyday use are popular, the absolute favorites for souvenirs are the Hello Kitty plush toys and mascots wearing "Kimono"! Packed with 100% Japanese charm and cuteness, these are guaranteed to bring a smile to any friend you gift them to.

- **Theme:** Japan-exclusive Sanrio goods and traditional-style character items
- **Store Name:** Sanrio Shinjuku Store
- **Hours:** 11:00 - 20:00
- **Location:** Kinokuniya Bookstore Shinjuku Main Store 1F (Meitengai), 3-17-7 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo
- **Google Maps:** <https://maps.app.goo.gl/YRKuRQowY49VZ8Pn8>

---

#### 3. Kiddy Land Shinjuku Store / Chiikawa Land

**From Mario to "Chiikawa" and Nostalgic Monchhichi—All in One Place!**

![](/images/columns/shinjuku-kawaii-vintage-shops/07.webp)

If you want to hunt for Japanese anime and video game character goods all in one place, the giant "Kiddy Land" (Okadaya Building) near Shinjuku Station's East Exit is the best choice. Their highly detailed Super Mario plush toys captivate fans of all ages from around the world.

![](/images/columns/shinjuku-kawaii-vintage-shops/08.webp)![](/images/columns/shinjuku-kawaii-vintage-shops/09.webp)![](/images/columns/shinjuku-kawaii-vintage-shops/10.webp)

A must-see on the 2nd floor is the specialty shop for "Chiikawa," which is currently experiencing a massive boom in Japan, practically becoming a social phenomenon! Furthermore, "Monchhichi," overwhelmingly nostalgic for older generations, is making a huge comeback. Monchhichi wearing traditional Japanese *hakama* and kimonos are truly rare souvenirs you can only buy here.

- **Theme:** A pop culture treasure trove featuring everything from trending characters to nostalgic favorites
- **Store Name:** Kiddy Land Shinjuku Store (Chiikawa Land, etc., are in the same building)
- **Hours:** 10:30 - 20:30 \*Irregular holidays
- **Location:** Okadaya Building 1F-4F, 3-23-17 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo
- **Google Maps:** <https://maps.app.goo.gl/DoL6f6tgVpNHJzFH9>

---

#### 4. Disney FLAGSHIP TOKYO

**Japan's Largest Disney Store! Rare Tokyo-Exclusive Items**

![](/images/columns/shinjuku-kawaii-vintage-shops/11.webp)

Facing Shinjuku Dori, this is Japan's largest flagship Disney Store. Spanning three floors from B1 to the 2nd floor, it is packed with Disney, Pixar, Star Wars, and Marvel merchandise.

![](/images/columns/shinjuku-kawaii-vintage-shops/12.webp)

For international Disney fans, the "Japan-exclusive" and "Tokyo-exclusive" designs are an absolute must-see. You can get your hands on rare "Kawaii" items that you can't even buy at the parks in the US, such as the cherry blossom season limited-edition pink Winnie the Pooh (Sakura Pooh) and Japanese-style Disney goods made in collaboration with traditional Japanese crafts. The magical interior design is also a sight to behold.

- **Theme:** Japan's largest Disney Store and Tokyo-exclusive items
- **Store Name:** Disney FLAGSHIP TOKYO
- **Hours:** 10:00 - 21:00
- **Location:** T&T III Building B1F-2F, 3-17-5 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo
- **Google Maps:** <https://maps.app.goo.gl/LEDmHtK74vk5y2yf6>
