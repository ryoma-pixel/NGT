faq:
- q: How much is the hanko, and when do I get it?
  a: 'The tour fee does not include the hanko. You pay directly at the shop (credit cards accepted): from about 3,300 JPY for a plastic seal and 10,000 JPY for premium materials, plus an express finishing fee (from 550 JPY). A hanko takes at least 60 minutes to make, so you pick up the finished seal yourself after the tour.'
- q: Do I need to buy anything?
  a: This is a walking tour; shopping and cafe expenses are not included.
- q: Can I bring my kids?
  a: Children 12 and under are free and very welcome (must be accompanied by a guardian). If a child wants their own hanko, the shop fee applies.
- q: What if it rains?
  a: Rain or shine. If it rains, we use covered streets and arcades as much as possible.
