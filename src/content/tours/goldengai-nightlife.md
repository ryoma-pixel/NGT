## What you’ll experience

### 1. Time travel into the “golden” maze
Just minutes from Shinjuku Station, the scenery changes completely. Tucked between modern skyscrapers lies Golden Gai, a rare wooden maze that survived Tokyo’s post-war redevelopment. More than 280 tiny bars, some seating only five people, are packed into its narrow alleys. Your Ninja guide shares how the area grew from its post-war roots as a black market. It feels like stepping back into old Tokyo.

### 2. Crack the code: how to pick a bar
Golden Gai can be intimidating for first-time visitors. Which door should you open? We turn it into a game: your Ninja guide challenges you to decode Japanese signboards and kanji, looking for clues in shapes, symbols and colors. Along the way you get insider tips on bars loved by anime fans, places welcoming to international guests and spots locals quietly recommend. By the end, you’ll feel confident enough to come back on your own.

### 3. An urban oasis: a shrine experience made easy
Suddenly, a bright vermilion torii gate appears in the middle of the city. This is Hanazono Shrine, the guardian shrine of Shinjuku’s entertainment district. Step inside and the noise fades away. We gently guide you through the Shinto ritual: washing your hands, bowing, clapping and making a wish. There is nothing to memorize.

### 4. Neon lights & Godzilla
For the finale, head into Kabukicho, the city that never sleeps. With your Ninja team you can enjoy its energy comfortably and safely, and we take you to the best spot for photos with the giant Godzilla Head. The tour ends near the popular nightclub WARP, so your night in Shinjuku is just getting started.
