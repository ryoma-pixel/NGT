## What you’ll experience

### 1. An artisan town watched over by the “Kappa”
Meet your local guide at Tawaramachi Station. A short walk from busy Senso-ji lies Kappabashi, the artisan district that supplies professional chefs. Start a scavenger hunt for hidden Kappa icons while you hear the legend of the mythical creatures that saved the town.

### 2. A one-coin treasure hunt at Dengama
First stop: Dengama, a wholesaler gathering ceramics from all over Japan. Wagons out front are piled with small plates and chopstick rests for just a few dollars. Learn the difference between earthenware and porcelain, and dig up treasures that fit easily in your suitcase.

### 3. The craftsmanship of Kiriko glass
Next, visit tsuchiya, a studio and shop for delicate Japanese cut glass called Kiriko. Compare the sharp cuts of Edo Kiriko, the color gradients of Satsuma Kiriko and the kaleidoscope reflections of Tenma Kiriko.

### 4. Choose your ultimate Japanese knife
Finally, visit a specialist knife shop that carries on the forging techniques of samurai swords. Depending on your taste, we take you to a traditional shop (Tsubaya) or a sleek modern store (Kama-Asa). Learn which materials suit beginners and have your name hand-engraved in Japanese katakana on the knife you buy. Your guide shares local dining tips while you wait for the engraving.

*Knife and item purchases are at your own expense.*
